other protocols on dreamstation.systems
=======================================

(more comprehensive explainer coming soon)

this box runs a few retro/fun/quirky internet services. here's everything it speaks:


  Gopher         gopher://dreamstation.systems
                 you're here

  Gemini/Spartan gemini://dreamstation.systems, spartan://dreamstation.systems
                 mostly the same content as this gopher hole, over Gemini or Spartan.

  DICT           dict://dreamstation.systems
                 a dictionary server (RFC 2229), port 2628.

  QOTD           in your terminal: nc dreamstation.systems 17
                 quote of the day (RFC 865), TCP/UDP port 17.

  Daytime        in your terminal: nc dreamstation.systems 13
                 the current date and time (RFC 867), TCP/UDP port 13.

  Echo           in your terminal: nc dreamstation.systems 7
                 whatever you type sent straight back to you (RFC 862), port 7.

  Discard        in your terminal: nc dreamstation.systems 9
                 everything you send is thrown away (RFC 863), port 9.

  Chargen        in your terminal: nc dreamstation.systems 19
                 an endless stream of ASCII (RFC 864), port 19. ctrl-C to escape.

  FTP            ftp://dreamstation.systems
                 an anonymous, read-only file archive.
