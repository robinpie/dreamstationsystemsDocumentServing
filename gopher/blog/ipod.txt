Photos from my 4th generation iPod Touch
========================================

I recently got a 4th generation iPod Touch just for taking 2010‐esque old‐digital photographs and I think it really goes hard. The crappy single‐focus sensor with the stock iOS 6 Camera app looks a lot different than modern smart‐device cameras.

This camera is clearly best when overwhelmed. Screens, bright lights, dim lights all turn out really unique‐looking, but well‐lit outdoor daytime pictures just look mediocre.
