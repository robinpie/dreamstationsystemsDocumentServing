unifont-1ceb0.woff2 -- a one-glyph subset (U+1CEB0) of GNU Unifont Upper 18.0.01
(https://unifoundry.com/unifont/), cut because none of the site's other fonts
have that codepoint (Unicode 16). Used by the .glyph-1ceb0 class in base.css.
Modified: subset to a single glyph, converted to WOFF2, hinting and layout
tables dropped.
