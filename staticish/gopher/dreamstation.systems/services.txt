other protocols on dreamstation.systems
=======================================

(more comprehensive explainer coming soon)

this box runs a few retro/fun/quirky internet services. here's everything it speaks:

Gemini / Spartan
----------------

identical content served on Gemini and Spartan.

Gopher
------

you’re here.

Finger
------

currently being set up. in your terminal: finger @dreamstation.systems

FTP
---

an anonymous, read-only file archive. plain FTP or explicit FTPS

ftp://dreamstation.systems: <ftp://dreamstation.systems>

NTP
---

part of pool.ntp.org. i also serve NTS on TCP/4460

NTP server stats (auto-regenerating): /0/ntpstats.txt

Email
-----

i also run a small personal-only email server here - email me at robin@dreamstation.systems 

DICT
----

a dictionary server (RFC 2229), port 2628.

in your favorite graphical dictd frontend, or in your terminal with dictd: dict -h dreamstation.systems -d jargon "amiga persecution complex" (or similar commands): <dict://dreamstation.systems>

QOTD
----

quote of the day (RFC 865), TCP/UDP port 17. in your terminal: nc dreamstation.systems 17

nc dreamstation.systems 17

Daytime
-------

the current date and time (RFC 867), TCP/UDP port 13. in your terminal: nc dreamstation.systems 13

nc dreamstation.systems 13

Echo
----

whatever you type sent straight back to you (RFC 862), TCP/UDP port 7. in your terminal: nc dreamstation.systems 7

nc dreamstation.systems 7

Discard
-------

everything you send is thrown away (RFC 863), TCP/UDP port 9. in your terminal: nc dreamstation.systems 9

nc dreamstation.systems 9

Chargen
-------

an endless stream of ASCII (RFC 864), TCP/UDP port 19. in your terminal, ctrl-C to escape: nc dreamstation.systems 19

nc dreamstation.systems 19
